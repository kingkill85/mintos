Frames for TV consoles, 640x480. From GermanTacos/overlays (MIT, see LICENSE-GermanTacos):
- nes-*.png: 640-480/640-480-int-fc-*.png as they are (window 512x480 at 64,0 = NES 2x)
- snes-*.png, sfc-*.png: 1024-768/1024-768-int-*.png scaled by 2/3 (Lanczos) and cut to
  640x480 at +21+16, so their 3x window becomes SNES 2x (512x448 at 64,16)
